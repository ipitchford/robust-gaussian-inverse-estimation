# Assurance and boundaries

The uniform regret theorem, covariance extension and jitter characterisations are analytic proofs in the manuscript. The finite checker stress-tests scalar dual identities and finite instances; it does not prove the uniform theorem. The two-band separation uses a standard-library exact rational certificate.

The supplied assessment and final publication checks were AI-assisted, not independent human peer review. No proof assistant was used.

All comparisons are among linear estimators and use normalized additive risk. No risk-ratio guarantee, estimator-matrix convergence, nonlinear minimax theorem, empirical-data validation or optimal `d^{-1/4}` rate claim is made.
