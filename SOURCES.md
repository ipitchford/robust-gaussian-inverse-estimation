# Sources and positioning

The complete bibliography is in `paper.tex`.

Krainovic, Soltanolkotabi and Heckel supply the spectral estimator formula, scalar dual representation and qualitative conjecture. The present work supplies the uniform quantitative proof and exact jitter boundary. Guan and Davenport's ICML 2025 paper studies iteration-wise noise injection for nonlinear model-based inverse reconstruction and develops average-case robustness theory; it does not supply this theorem. Robust-state-estimation, Wasserstein and covariance-estimation papers cited in the manuscript concern distinct uncertainty models or quantities.

Generic trust-region duality, Gaussian concentration and spectral calculus are methods, not claimed inventions.
